WE DESIGN SPACES THAT BECOME EXPERIENCES.

NOVA is a women-led architecture and interior design studio founded in Mérida, Yucatán, (México) and shaped by a global perspective. We approach every project as an opportunity to create something beyond the physical—to design environments that invite interaction, movement, and connection.

Our work is driven by curiosity, innovation, and a constant search for new ways of experiencing space. From the first idea to the smallest detail, we believe architecture should feel intentional, unexpected, and alive.
